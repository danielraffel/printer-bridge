Welcome to Printer Bridge

Printer Bridge helps keep older printers useful by enabling AirPrint for printers that already work with a modern Mac.

If your Mac can already print to a printer but it does not support AirPrint, Printer Bridge will usually make it available to iPhone, iPad, and other Apple devices on your local network.

The app installs to /Applications. After installation, launch Printer Bridge once to finish setup and enable background sharing.
